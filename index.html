Marketplace Rules

  1. Use Steam-hosted login onlyNever share a Steam password, Steam Guard code, recovery code, or session cookie with a buyer or seller.
  2. List only owned, tradable itemsA public Steam inventory is required so each listing can be verified against the connected account.
  3. Keep bidding fairSelf-bidding, coordinated price manipulation, fake offers, and misleading listing details are not allowed.
  4. Check every tradeVerify the recipient, item, condition, and complete Steam trade contents before accepting an offer or sending payment.
  5. No scams or account abuseImpersonation, phishing, harassment, fraudulent chargebacks, and alternate-account abuse can result in marketplace removal.

The marketplace currently records public listings and bids. It does not process payments, hold funds, or guarantee a Steam trade.
